About CCGE-2026

The 2nd International Conference on Computing, Communication and Green Engineering (CCGE-2026) aims to bring together leading academic scientists, researchers and scholars to exchange and share their experiences and research results on all aspects of Computing, Communication and Green Engineering. It also provides a premier interdisciplinary platform for researchers, practitioners and educators to present and discuss the most recent innovations, trends, and concerns as well as practical challenges encountered and solutions adopted in the fields of Computing, Communication and Green Engineering.

About RSCOE

JSPM's Rajarshi Shahu College of Engineering (RSCOE) is an autonomous institute affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University. Established in 2001, RSCOE has consistently been at the forefront of providing quality technical education. The college is known for its academic excellence, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and strong industry-academia linkages. With numerous national and international accolades, RSCOE continues to nurture young engineers and researchers, preparing them to tackle global challenges through innovation and sustainability.

Conference Scope

The conference welcomes papers in the following areas (but not limited to):

Track 1: Green Computing & AI

Energy-efficient IT solutions, sustainable data centers, green software engineering, and AI/ML applications for environmental sustainability and resource optimization.

Track 2: Advanced Communication

5G/6G network energy efficiency, IoT & wireless sensor networks, next-gen optical communications, and secure networked systems for green societies.

Track 3: Smart Systems & Energy

Smart grid technologies, renewable energy integration, intelligent transportation systems, smart cities, and sustainable building automation.

Track 4: Electronics & IoT

Energy harvesting circuits, low-power VLSI design, embedded systems for sustainability, and industrial automation for energy conservation.

Publication
