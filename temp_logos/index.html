About The Conference

The 2nd International Conference on Computing, Communication and Green Engineering (CCGE-2026) aims to bring together leading academic scientists, researchers and scholars to exchange and share their experiences and research results on all aspects of Computing, Communication and Green Engineering. It also provides a premier interdisciplinary platform for researchers, practitioners and educators to present and discuss the most recent innovations, trends, and concerns as well as practical challenges encountered and solutions adopted in the fields of Computing, Communication and Green Engineering.

Conference Scope

The conference welcomes papers in the following areas (but not limited to):

Green Computing

Green computing and energy-efficient IT solutions for sustainable technology development.

Cloud Computing

Cloud computing and sustainable data centers for efficient resource utilization.

Cyber Security

Cyber security in the era of digital transformation and emerging threats.

5G Networks

Advanced 5G network technologies, applications, and security considerations.

Sustainable IT Practices

Sustainable IT practices for reducing environmental impact and carbon footprint.

AI for Sustainability

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning applications for sustainability.

Publication

All accepted and presented papers will be forwarded for the possible inclusion in IEEE Xplore.
